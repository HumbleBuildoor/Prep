# Roadmap

Kept short on purpose. The personal app stays simple; the larger survival-companion concept is a separate product.

## Now
- Water: drinking and non-drinking, treatment, and what the questionnaire answers mean for each scenario.

## Next
- A guided start for a new household: questionnaire, first item by photo, then backup. Needed before sharing the app with other households.
- The questionnaire's location kinds used by the location suggestions.
- Plan and emergency mode: a plan per scenario and first-hour checklists.

## Later
- Medical, hygiene and kit areas, switched off for now.
- A QR code for pairing devices.
- Rolling back a bad change from the relay's history.
- Documents, cash and contacts, with a printable card.
- Offline guides, then alerts.

## Not in this app
- Seeing another household's supplies, groups of households, trade, shared maps. These belong to the separate survival-companion product.
