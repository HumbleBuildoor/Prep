# Roadmap

Kept short on purpose. The personal app stays simple; the larger survival-companion concept is a separate product.

The app has two modes. **Prep mode** is for getting and staying ready. **Live mode** is for getting through something that is happening now. Both run on the same supplies and the same calculation.

## Done
1. **To do list.** Gaps, open questions and dates in one ranked list, drinking water first. Ready shows the next three actions.
2. **Water.** Drinking and washing apart; sealed, filled, washing and treatment kinds; three levels (Bare minimum, Basic, Comfortable); a tap that can be unsafe; a power cut that reaches the taps; one sealed day held back.
3. **Live mode, water cut.** Now, State, Supplies and Log; steps in order; one tap for the day's water; an end that turns what was used into a restock list. Synced across devices.
4. **Power cut, both modes.** Cooking by how the household cooks; a stove only with somewhere ventilated; fuel shared between boiling and cooking; the fridge and freezer clock; no counting on shops for the first days.

## Next, in order
5. **People and pets.** Every person counted, children included. Dogs by weight.
6. **Events that change.** Stages (warning, event, recovery), "cannot go outside", haze with windows shut, one resupply rule for every need. Flood as the first staged scenario.
7. **Leaving.** What the bags are for, what each adult can carry, the vehicle's range and reserve, a destination. Live mode away from home.
8. **Power in detail, and the rest.** Devices and their watts instead of a flat figure, the car and solar as sources, power banks not checked shown as unverified, dates checked against a scenario's length, triggers.

## Also wanted
- A guided start for a new household: questionnaire, first item by photo, then backup. Needed before sharing the app with other households.
- Live mode: daily use for food, fuel and power as well as water; Supplies tuned for using.
- The questionnaire's location kinds used by the location suggestions.

## Later
- Medical, hygiene (toilets included) and kit areas, switched off for now.
- A QR code for pairing devices.
- Rolling back a bad change from the relay's history.
- Documents, cash and contacts, with a printable card.
- Offline guides, then alerts.

## Not in this app
- Seeing another household's supplies, groups of households, trade, shared maps. These belong to the separate survival-companion product.
